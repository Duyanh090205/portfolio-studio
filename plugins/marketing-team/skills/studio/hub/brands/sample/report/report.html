08 · Insights and recommendations

Key insights

  1. Engagement rate rose from 6.0% to 6.8% while reach grew 33%, so the swirl-and-sharing story is landing with a wider audience, not only the launch crowd.
  2. The "Bring The Group" promo post was the single highest post of the month for both reach (24,600) and engagements (2,150), confirming the group-offer angle is the strongest pillar right now.
  3. New-follower growth cooled 31% off the launch spike, expected after an opening month, but rising engagement per follower shows the base warming up rather than the account stalling.

Recommendations

  1. Plan at least one more "Bring The Group" style offer post next month; it is the best-performing content so far.
  2. Instagram's engagement rate (7.4%) is well ahead of Facebook's (5.7%): shift more story and photo content there, and keep Facebook focused on the group-offer angle.
  3. Follower growth is slowing, so pair the next launch-style moment (a new flavour) with a small paid boost or a local collab to bring in fresh top-of-funnel reach.
